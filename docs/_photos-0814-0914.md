## Technical findings

### Serial link between PC and control box — it is RS-485, not RS-232
- **IMG_0902** (schematic "KOMMUNIKATION RS485", =1DA0.M1 sheet 2, dated 30.11.92): the PC hosts an ISA plug-in card **Y4A 013 000** ("FC-Einschub RS 485" per parts list IMG_0892, i.e. PC-card RS-485). Its connector −A1-X2 uses only three pins: **pin 2 = GND/SIGN, pin 3 = DATA−, pin 8 = DATA+** (wire colors cy/ws/rs = ?gray?/white/red). Cable **K1000** runs to bulkhead connector **+1DL1-X300** — this is the DB9 visible on the red box rear (IMG_0914) — then cable **K1010** continues to the CPU board (Y4A012000/Y4A015000) connector **−A2-X2000** with identical pinout (2 GND/SIGN, 3 DATA−, 8 DATA+). Cable **K1020** daisy-chains from CPU −A2-X1000 to the SPS 2000 board −A3-X201, same three pins. Bus termination/bias networks at both ends (PC card −A1-X1000 and SPS −A3-X280): **330 Ω 0.3 W from +5 V (pin 9) to DATA+ (pin 8), 120 Ω 0.3 W between DATA+ and DATA− (pin 3), 330 Ω 0.3 W from DATA− to GND (pin 5)** (resistors R4/R5/R6, Philips MRS25 330E and 120E per parts lists IMG_0892/0893).
- So the DB9 on the box is a **3-wire half-duplex RS-485 multidrop bus** (PC card ↔ axis-controller CPU ↔ SPS PLC), with +5 V and GND also on pins 9 and 5 at the terminated ends.

### Serial parameters of the DOS software (EMCO Fanuc-0T emulation)
- **IMG_0882** (classroom guide p.80, "Parameters of serial interfaces" screen): COM 1 / COM 2 / DNC all default to **Baudrate 9600, Parity E (even), Stopbits 1, Databits 7, Control parameter 00000001**. Text: "Even parity, 1 stopbit, 7 databits the standard setting"; baud must match the connected device. (These govern external program upload/download from the DOS PC, distinct from the internal RS-485 machine bus.)
- **IMG_0880** (p.78): transmittable data = single or all CNC programs, plus offsets (wear and geometry, zero point shift). Device letters typed at ENTRY: **1 = COM1, 2 = COM2, a = DRVA: (floppy A), b = DRVB:, c = DRVC: (hard disk), P = LPT1**.
- **IMG_0881** (p.79): device selected on Fanuc SETTING screen field `I/O =`; SETTING 1 shows TVSW=0, ISO=1 (0:EIA 1:ISO), INCH=1, SEQZ=1.
- **IMG_0883–0886**: punch/read procedures — OUTPUT START to unload/print program(s), INPUT to load, OFFSET menu + INPUT/OUTPUT START for offsets ("transmit always all of tool data wear, geometry and zero point shift").

### Control box board set (red box, per mounting-panel drawing IMG_0897 and schematics)
- **=1DA0.M1-A2 "Axiscontroller" (CPU board) = Y4A 012 000** with plug-on **Y4A 015 000**; handwritten note on IMG_0897: "former CPU being replaced". This — not an "AC95" — is what the 93-5 electrical doc calls the brain. It carries: X104 (16-pin ribbon, step/dir for both axes), X107 (14-pin, spindle FRC control), X109 (spindle encoder), X110/X111 (E-stop chain), X113/X114 (X ref + sync switches), X117/X118 (Z ref + sync switches), X1000/X2000 (RS-485).
- **=1DC0.M1-A1 stepper motor board = Y4A 018 000** ("Schrittmotorplatine", drives both 5-phase steppers; confirms the community part number Y4A018000).
- **=1DA0.M1-A3 SPS 2000 PLC board = Y4A 011 000** + connector board **Y4A 016 000** (SPS-Steckerplatine).
- **=1DA0.M1-A5 DC/DC converter = Y4A 014 000** ("G.NETZTEIL RS 485" in the parts list; makes +5 V, ±12 V). Pin groups on −A5-X1 → −A2-X9 → −A3-X11 ribbon: 5 VDC pins 2,24,25,26; GND 3,4,22,23; +12 V 10,17; GND 11,16; −12 V 12,15; GND 13,14 (IMG_0901).
- **=1DB1.U1-A1 main drive board = Y4A 400 000** ("G.FREQUENZUMRICHTER FRC105", frequency inverter for the spindle; matches community Y4A400000). On-card fuses 4 AFF and 100 mAT (IMG_0898/0903).
- **=1DD1.U1-A1 tool turret board = Y4A 017 000** (G.Werkzeugwenderplatine); **=1DD1.H1-B1 turret encoder board = Y4A 020 000** (G.Drehgeberplatine WZW).
- **PC side: Y4A 013 000 = FC-Einschub 485** (ISA card in the DOS PC).
- Fuses on the mounting panel (IMG_0897/0899/0901): **F1 2.5 AT SPS outputs, F2 2.5 AT SPS inputs, F3 2.5 AT axiscontroller, F4 6.3 AT 24 V supply**; glass fuses 2.5 A T 6.3×32 (Wickmann) per parts list. Terminal strips: **X1 = 230 V strip, X2 = 24 V strip**.

### Power architecture (IMG_0898, 0900, 0899, 0910, 0913)
- Mains: **110/230 V 1/N/PE 50/60 Hz** into fused IEC socket (−F1 6.3 AT), key main switch −S2 (Telemecanique lock key switch), then transformer **−T1: 600 VA, primary 110–230 V with taps 100/120/220/240 V, secondary 18 V 7 A** (Katronik single-phase). Handwritten note (IMG_0898): "Transformer steps 110 V to 230 V" (autotransformer duty for US use); 230 V bus feeds the FRC spindle inverter and two Papst 4580N 230 V 0.125 A fans.
- 24 V rail: 18 V secondary → bridge rectifier ZEG212210 (100 V 10 A) → **15000 µF/40 V** electrolytic → −F4 6.3 AT → 24 VDC bus feeding stepper board, CPU board, SPS 2000, E-OFF chain and turret motor (IMG_0899/0900).
- Main-drive contactors: 2× ABB **BC6-30-01 DC contactors, 24 VDC coils** (K1, K2) switch 230 V to the FRC card (IMG_0893/0898).

### Axis drives (IMG_0894, 0899, 0905–0908)
- Axis motors X and Z: **Berger Lahr 5-phase steppers VRDM 564/50LN**, terminal box, **rated current 0.95 A, Schrittzahl 1000/500 steps/rev (half/full step)**, Berger Lahr order no. 12670015000. Pentagon winding W1–W5 drawn in IMG_0905/0907 (schematic symbol "M 5~").
- Stepper board Y4A018000 front: **SERVO READY LEDs for X, Z, Y**; DIP switch **S301 (1–4): VOLL/HALB (full/half step) + three current-set switches**; jumper field **X202 1–4 "R-LAUF"**. Motor connectors X301 (X axis) and X501 (Z axis), 6 wires each.
- Step/direction interface: 16-pin ribbon **K1040** from CPU board **−A2-X104** to stepper board −A1-X201. Signals: **pin 1 SR X (servo ready), 2 DIR X, 3 DIR X/NEG, 4 CK X, 5 CK X/NEG; pin 6 SR Z, 7 DIR Z, 8 DIR Z/NEG, 9 CK Z, 10 CK Z/NEG, 16 E-Achse** (differential direction/clock pairs per axis). This corrects the community note: X104 is on the *CPU/axiscontroller* board (Y4A012000) and the ribbon carries both axes to the stepper power card Y4A018000.

### Homing / limit sensing (IMG_0894, 0906, 0908)
- Per axis: one **reference switch** (mechanical; parts list shows Schmersal ZS 236-11Z end switches and an Omron V-10FL2-1C2 basic switch, plus Schmersal ZR231-11Y roller lever) wired to CPU inputs X113 (X, input I1 24 VDC) / X117 (Z, input I3 24 VDC), and one **"Sync-Bero" inductive proximity switch, Balluff BES 516-343-E0-X, NPN, 12–24 V, N.O.** wired 3-wire (24 VDC/GND/signal SYXS·SYZS) to CPU X114 (X) / X118 (Z). So homing = mechanical reference switch + inductive sync sensor per axis (the sync-Bero likely marks one motor revolution).
- Emergency-off circuit (IMG_0911): series chain of **E-stop (Telemecanique ZA2 BS54), gear-box cover switch, door end switch** feeding CPU board X110/X111 and relays K1/K2 (1N4007 flyback diodes) that drop the main-drive contactors.

### Spindle (IMG_0893, 0898, 0903, 0904)
- Motor: **ELIN IEC standard motor, 0.37 kW, 1370 rpm, 220/380 V 3-phase**, frame 71, B14 flange, IP54 (Best.Nr. LKM607N0433B). Driven by the **FRC105 frequency inverter card Y4A400000** from the 230 V bus; contactor pair K1/K2 in front of the card.
- Control interface FRC ↔ CPU X107 (14-way): differential pairs **E/AMD± (enable?), DIR MD± (spindle direction), CLK MD± (speed clock)**, plus **NI± (zero pulse), IA MD, GND, SR M± (servo ready)**.
- **A spindle encoder exists**: schematic IMG_0904 item 3 "Encoder" (+1DU1-B1, drawn as a speed/pulse pickup) connects via K3040 to CPU board **X109: GND, 5VDC, STROBE, SYNC**. The parts list (IMG_0893) lists "Drehgeberplatten für EMCO-Hauptantrieb" (encoder plates/board for the main drive, handwritten "Encoder Board for EMCO Main drive", part R3D42300x). So threading-capable spindle feedback (at least a sync/index plus strobe channel) is present in the design.

### Tool turret (IMG_0894, 0899, 0912)
- Motor: **Maxon 24 VDC gearmotor with 60:1 spur gearhead** (combination no. 41.023.038-00.00-000, motor 2023.916-21.581-000, gearhead 2938.804-0060.0-000), driven one-way from SPS output **A0.2 "Werkzeugwender vorwärts" (turret forward)**.
- Position sensing: turret encoder board **Y4A 020 000** ("Drehgeberplatine WZW") powered +5 V/GND from SPS analog connector X221, returning **Strobe (E0.0, X212:1)** and **Sync (E0.1, X212:2)** signals (IMG_0912). Classic EMCO ratchet turret: forward-run + strobe/sync counting, locks by reversing against a pawl (reverse not shown on this sheet; only "vorwärts" labeled).

### Machine data gleaned from the training binder
- **Rapid traverse 29.5 inch/min** (= 750 mm/min), stated in the path-optimization example (IMG_0875).
- **SMAX 3000** shown on program CHECK screen (IMG_0863/0865) — 3000 rpm spindle limit; adjustment sheet uses 500–660 ft/min constant surface speed and 600 rpm threading (IMG_0834).
- Feed override dial 0–120 % (IMG_0861). Tool measurement gauge length 1.1811 inch = 30 mm (IMG_0826).
- Control emulation: screens titled "GE Fanuc Series 0-T"; software "SYSTEM EDITION 0465-15" with ~302 kB free program memory (IMG_0877).
- G/M-code sets (IMG_0814–0816) are the EMCO Fanuc-0T dialect: G20/G21/G24 single cycles, G71–G78 compound cycles, G92 speed clamp, G96/G97, M20/21 tailstock, M25/26 clamping device, M71/72 blow-off, M98/99 subprograms.

### Cabling machine ↔ box (from schematics; cable numbers)
- K1000 (PC card → box DB9 X300), K1010/K1020 (internal RS-485), K1030 (CPU X107 → FRC X1, 14-way), K1040 (CPU X104 → stepper board X201, 16-way ribbon), K3040 (spindle encoder), K3050/K3080 (X/Z stepper motor cables, 6-way), K3060/K3090 (X/Z sync-Bero), K3070/K3100 (X/Z reference switch), K3000/K3010/K3020 (E-stop chain). Machine-side connectors ride on +1DL1-X2 bulkhead terminal.

## Uncertain

- IMG_0914 type plate: "PC TURN 50" and field labels (V / Hz / kW) are visible but values are unreadable at this resolution; upper label read as "A6A 911 000 06B" — needs a closer photo for serial number and year.
- The 18 V transformer secondary current: parts list (IMG_0891) says "SEKUNDÄR 18V 7A" and schematic IMG_0900 says "Sekundär: 18V 7A" — but the 24 V-supply sheet (IMG_0899) marks the rectifier input "18V/1.3", and IMG_0898 "18V/2.1"; these are probably sheet/path cross-references, not amps. Rated 7 A is the safer reading.
- Turret motor part digits (41.023.038-00.00-000 etc., IMG_0894) are from a slightly blurred dot-matrix print; double-check before ordering.
- ELIN spindle motor order number LKM607N0433B partially blurred (IMG_0893).
- CPU X107 signal names E/AMD, IA MD (IMG_0904) hard to read; could be "F/AMD", "1A MD". The general pattern (differential enable/dir/clk + zero pulse NI + ready SR M) is solid.
- Spindle encoder channel count: only STROBE + SYNC (plus 5 V/GND) appear at CPU X109. Whether this is a multi-line quadrature encoder or a few-pulse + index pickup cannot be determined from these sheets; the turret uses the same Strobe/Sync naming.
- The X-axis control sheet (IMG_0906) is slightly blurred; pin order of X104 pins 1–5 (SR X first) inferred together with the sharper Z sheet (IMG_0908).
- IMG_0835 second-clamping zero shift read as Z −3.287 (with a second value −1.767); blurry.
- Classroom-guide alarm behavior: only one alarm example ("2631 N 15 Position already programmed", IMG_0858) — the binder's full alarm list was not in this photo range.
- Handwritten annotation on IMG_0906 "inductive proximity safety switch normally closed??" conflicts with the parts list "SCHLIESSER" (N.O.) for the BES 516-343; the parts list is printed and probably right.
